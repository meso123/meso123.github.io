04 / COMPUTER VISION / SOLO ANALYSIS

Edges & Lines

Classical image processing tested against fluorescent cells, ground truth and noisy architectural geometry.

TOOLSPython, OpenCV, NumPy, SciPy, scikit-image
METHODSSobel, Prewitt, Laplacian, Canny and probabilistic Hough
FOCUSParameter sensitivity and evidence-led comparison
Read report ↗

A threshold is a decision, not a magic number.

The work compared how classical operators respond to very different visual conditions. HEp-2 cell images made weak fluorescence and ground-truth agreement important; the University of Birmingham Dubai building made structural line selection the challenge.

Cell boundaries

Sobel and Prewitt preserved more low-intensity boundaries, while Canny produced the thinnest responses but could miss weaker edges. The useful conclusion was not that one detector always wins—it was how the input characteristics and evaluation target change the choice.

Architectural lines

An untuned probabilistic Hough transform returned 1,075 noisy segments. Systematic parameter adjustment reduced this to 13 structural detections that better represented the building rather than every local edge.

Good computer vision is not just an operator. It is the reasoned connection between image, parameter and evaluation target.
BEFORE1,075 noisy line detections
AFTER13 structural lines

What the experiments showed.

  • Thin edges are not automatically complete edges.
  • Ground truth makes threshold trade-offs inspectable rather than subjective.
  • Visualising intermediate results is essential for parameter tuning.
  • A smaller, more meaningful output is often the better result.
